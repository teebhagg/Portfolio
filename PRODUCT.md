# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, engineering leads, prospective clients, and peers reviewing Joshua Ansah's software engineering work.

## Product Purpose

Present Joshua's skills, experience, projects, and writing clearly, help visitors evaluate his work, and give them a direct way to contact him or leave feedback.

## Brand Personality

Warm, catchy, premium. The site should feel personal and confident while keeping its existing monochrome identity.

## Anti-references

Generic portfolio templates, fabricated testimonials, abrupt decorative motion, inconsistent rounded surfaces, and interactions that hide or clip content.

## Design Principles

- Make project work and professional experience easy to scan.
- Keep the existing monochrome visual identity consistent across routes and themes.
- Use smooth, purposeful animation with clear interaction feedback.
- Keep visitor feedback transparent about local-only behavior until a backend exists.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Ensure keyboard operation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and a restrained motion alternative when the visitor's system requests reduced motion.
